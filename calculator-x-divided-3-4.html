Power Conversion Calculator

Calculate the conversion factor between a measured power value and the power percentage stored in the machine.

Section A — Calculate Stored Machine Power

Use this section when you know the found power percentage and want to calculate the power percentage that must be stored in the machine.

Y = 3400 / X
Z = %P × Y
Conversion factor and stored power will appear here.

Section B — Recover Found Power

Use this section when you know the power percentage stored in the machine and want to recover the corresponding found power percentage.

Y = 3400 / X
%P = Z / Y
Conversion factor and found power will appear here.

Section A and Section B operate independently.
